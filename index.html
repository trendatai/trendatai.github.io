Tips & Tricks — Workflow Hacks

Image-to-Video Workflow

Never generate video directly from text; craft a high-detail base image in Midjourney first, then animate it in Runway for 10x fidelity.

Precise Camera Values

Always set numeric values (e.g. Pan Right +2) rather than descriptive words to easily stitch consecutive cuts in post-production.
